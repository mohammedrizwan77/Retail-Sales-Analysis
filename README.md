Retail Sales Analysis

End-to-end analysis of retail sales data (2023–2025) for a client, from SQL queries to a Power BI dashboard and a written report.

Key results

Total sales 2.13M, profit 219.46K, 4,998 orders, 1,180 customers, 10.3% overall margin
Sales growth slowed from +13.7% (2024) to +4.3% (2025)
Technology is 75% of sales but has the lowest margin (8.5%); Laptop (5.3%) and Smartphone (8.5%) are the main drag
Office Supplies has the highest margin (33.5%)
Chennai and Kolkata have the weakest city margins

Workflow

SQL (MySQL): aggregated sales, profit and quantity by segment, region, city, category, product and year
Python: duplicate and null checks, filling missing values, datatype conversion, and visuals with Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn
Power BI: interactive dashboard with slicers for segment, category, year, ship mode and payment type
Report: findings and recommendations

Tools: MySQL, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Power BI
